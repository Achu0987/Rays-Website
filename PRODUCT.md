# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

> Sourced from the owner-supplied developer pack (docs/) rather than an interview: the owner declined the init questions on 2026-10-30 and asked us to proceed. Facts below are taken from those documents; anything inferred is marked *(inferred)*.

## Platform

web

## Users
- A curious stranger arriving from social media or search, often on a phone, who should leave thinking "who is this person? I want to explore."
- A potential partner or investor checking whether the ambition is grounded in real work.
- Readers and followers returning for essays and stories.
- Media looking for a biography, approved photos and a contact route.

## Product Purpose
RayMindset.com is the digital home of Rayansh "Ray" Singh: an evolving, cinematic world covering the person, ideas, journey, work and unwritten chapters. Success means a visitor understands within seconds who Ray is, explores at least one chapter, and knows how to get in touch.

## Positioning
"Welcome to Ray's Mind": a living record of one person in motion (builder, boxer, traveller, writer), not a finished success story, agency portfolio, guru funnel or luxury flex site. Socialsect is one chapter, and Ray's identity is the umbrella.

## Operating Context
Homepage chapters: Portal, Identity, Journey, Builder (Socialsect), Fighter (boxing), Explorer (travel and *The Suitcase of Memories*), Ideas, Unwritten Future, Connect. Later routes: /story, /ventures, /journal, /experiences, /media, /connect, /privacy. Content is CMS-driven (CMS not chosen yet).

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Next.js 16 App Router, JavaScript only (no TypeScript), Tailwind v4, Three.js + React Three Fiber.
- Must be fully usable without WebGL or motion. The intro is skippable, with no scroll hijacking and no autoplaying sound.
- Mobile is first-class; heavy 3D gets a lighter variant on weak devices.
- Performance targets: LCP ≤ 2.5s, INP ≤ 200ms, CLS ≤ 0.1.
- No GitHub, commits or deploys until the owner approves.
- Open: CMS, licensed typefaces, canonical domain.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Rayansh Singh, uses "Ray". Draft hero line: "Welcome to Ray's Mind." (needs Ray's approval).
- Homepage concept: not set. Earlier concepts were removed on 2026-10-30 at the owner's request; the owner will supply the new idea.
- Owner requirement: everything must look **real**. Use real 3D models, real photographs and real footage. Stylized or procedural CG was rejected three times. If Ray's own material is missing, use real, openly licensed material from the web, credited and labelled as a placeholder.
- Voice: first person, direct, curious, self-aware. No hustle clichés or "visionary" language.

## Evidence on Hand
- None of Ray's own photos, footage, timeline or book cover is in the project yet.
- Must not be fabricated: biography facts, dates, titles, press, clients, numbers, endorsements, or any picture presented as Ray.
- Must be framed as ambition, never achievement: future ventures such as hospitals, healthcare products or wealth.

## Product Principles
1. Story before spectacle: every visual has a narrative job.
2. Credibility over hype: say only what can be proven.
3. Real over rendered: real material beats invented CG.
4. Content survives without the show: text and actions never depend on WebGL.
5. The site grows with Ray: chapters are content, not hardcoded art.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast; keyboard and touch parity; skip link; prefers-reduced-motion honoured with a static variant; semantic HTML alongside every canvas; no information conveyed only through 3D.
